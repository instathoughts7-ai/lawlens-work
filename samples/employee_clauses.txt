EMPLOYMENT TERMS & CONDITIONS ADDENDUM

1. Probation & Confirmation:
The employee will remain on probation until confirmed in writing by the management, and the probation may be extended from time to time at the company's discretion.

2. Intellectual Property Assignment:
The employee assigns all prior inventions and all intellectual property conceived, whether or not during working hours, exclusively to the company.

3. Joining Bonus & Training Cost Recovery:
In the event the employee leaves within 18 months, the company reserves the right to clawback and demand repayment of joining bonus and recover all training costs incurred.

4. Deductions & Recoveries:
The company may unilaterally deduct from salary any damage, loss, or shortfall, and any shortfall in notice will be deducted from gross salary. Furthermore, a portion of salary will be retained as security.

5. Resignation & Exit Acceptance:
Any resignation tendered by the employee is strictly subject to acceptance by the company, and the company reserves the right to reject the resignation.
