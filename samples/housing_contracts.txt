RESIDENTIAL LEASE AND APARTMENT BUYER AGREEMENT

1. Security Deposit:
The tenant shall deposit a sum of INR 1,00,000. In the event of early termination or lease dispute, the entire deposit will be forfeited entirely at landlord discretion.

2. Lock-in Period & Exit:
The lease includes a mandatory 12-month lock-in period. The tenant cannot vacate during lock-in, and if vacating early, shall pay rent for entire unexpired lock-in period.

3. Maintenance & Repairs:
The tenant shall bear all repairs and the tenant is responsible for structural repairs and seepage issues within the premises during tenancy.

4. Builder-Buyer Possession Timeline:
The promoter shall deliver possession by December 2026, with a grace period of 6 months without compensation. The builder is not liable for any delay and no interest is payable for delayed possession.

5. Payment Plan & Forfeiture:
In the event of cancellation, the promoter reserves the right to forfeit the entire earnest money deposit, and forfeiture of 20% of total consideration shall apply.
