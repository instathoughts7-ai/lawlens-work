MEMORANDUM OF FAMILY SETTLEMENT AND AGREEMENT

1. Family Settlement & Relinquishment:
Person B unconditionally gives up all rights to ancestral property and relinquishes all future claims without any consideration. Furthermore, the parties agree that Person A retains sole discretion to modify the settlement terms.

2. Partition & Division of Assets:
The properties described herein are mutually partitioned between the co-sharers as agreed, with absolute rights assigned to respective parties.

3. Maintenance Agreement:
Person A agrees to provide monthly maintenance to Person B, provided that maintenance shall cease at the sole discretion of Person A should any dispute arise.

4. Deed of Gift:
The donor conveys the described movable assets, provided that the donor reserves the right to revoke this gift at will at any time.
