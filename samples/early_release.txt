EARLY RELEASE AND NOTICE BUYOUT CLAUSE

1. Early Release & Buyout Discretion: An employee wishing to be relieved before completing the full stipulated notice period may submit a written request for early release or notice buyout. Any early release or buyout of the notice period is subject to the sole and absolute discretion of the Company and cannot be claimed as a matter of right. The Company reserves the right to reject any buyout request without assigning any reason.

2. Financial Recovery & Buyout Terms: In the event that early release is approved at the management's discretion, the Company reserves the right to recover gross salary for the unserved notice period along with reimbursement of any replacement recruitment expenses and project handover costs.

3. Exit Formalities & Documentation: The Company reserves the right to withhold the Relieving Letter and Experience Certificate until full clearance of all handover formalities, asset return, and settlement of buyout dues.
