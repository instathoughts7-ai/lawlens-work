OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT & APPOINTMENT TERMS

Dear Rahul,

We are pleased to offer you employment with Apex Innovations Pvt Ltd on the following terms:

1. Compensation & Variable Pay:
Your total Cost to Company (CTC) will be INR 18,00,000 per annum. In addition to your fixed base salary, you will be eligible for an annual performance-linked variable pay of up to 15% of your annual CTC, payable in accordance with the Company's annual performance bonus policy.

2. Non-Compete & Restrictive Covenants:
During your employment with the Company and for a duration of 12 months following termination or resignation, you shall not engage in, assist, or provide services to any competing business or direct competitor within India.

3. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property:
You acknowledge that you will have access to confidential proprietary information. You agree to maintain strict confidentiality and assign all intellectual property and inventions conceived during your employment to the Company.

4. Termination & Notice:
Either party may terminate this employment relationship by giving 60 days' written notice or payment of basic salary in lieu of notice.
